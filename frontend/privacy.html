Privacy Policy

Deep Sweep (deepsweep.pkresearch.in) · Last updated 25 September 2026

Deep Sweep is a research dashboard for Indian and US listed companies, available on the web and as an Android/iOS app. This page explains what data the app and website collect, what they're used for, and who to contact about them.

What we collect

  • Account details — the name and email address you sign up with, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Your watchlists, saved screens and preferences — stored against your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Messages you send us — anything submitted through "Write to us", together with your name/email if you were logged in.
  • Push notification device token (mobile app only) — a token issued by Apple/Google identifying your installed copy of the app, used only to deliver notifications you're already logged in to receive (e.g. News Channel updates). The website has no equivalent; only the installed app registers one.
  • Basic server logs — standard web server logs (IP address, timestamp, page requested) kept for security and debugging, not linked to your account.

What we don't collect

No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no location data, no contacts or photos, and no data is sold to anyone.

How it's used

Solely to run the dashboard: to log you in, show your watchlist and saved screens on whichever device you use, respond to messages you send, and (in the app) deliver the push notifications you're signed up for. Push notifications are sent via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google); no notification content is shared with Google beyond what's needed to deliver it.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. It is not shared with third parties except the infrastructure needed to run the service (hosting and, for the mobile app, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery).

Data retention & deletion

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and associated data, or to remove a push notification registration, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Deep Sweep is a financial research tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
