BOARD DISCLOSURES

Board Disclosures

SEC filings for companies on the US board -- the closest free, automated US equivalent to India's Bulk & Block Deals and Announcements, but genuinely different signals: NSE names the counterparty on any large trade and publishes a whole-exchange announcements feed; the US publishes neither. Insider Transactions (Form 4) only covers insiders' own trades; Announcements (Form 8-K) only covers this board's own companies, not the wider market.

Every officer, director and 10%+ owner buy/sell disclosure, filed within two business days of the trade. "Purchase" and "Sale" are real open-market trades; other types (option exercises, tax withholding, awards, gifts) are also insider activity but not a market buy/sell.
